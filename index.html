Welcome to Marketing the Arts

A brilliant exhibition or a gutsy new play can still end up playing to an empty room. Marketing is how you fix that, and this course shows you how.

You’ll learn the fundamentals of marketing, explore what makes arts and culture marketing its own creative animal, and get comfortable with market research. You’ll also learn to build strategies that grow audiences and increase accessibility.

Through case studies, readings, lively discussion, and hands-on exercises using real organizations and this week’s headlines, you’ll practice turning first-time visitors into loyal fans.

You’ll leave with practical skills you can use right away and a new way of seeing every poster, email, and Instagram post that crosses your path.
