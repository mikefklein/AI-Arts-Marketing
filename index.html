Welcome to Marketing the Arts

A brilliant exhibition or a gutsy new play can still end up playing to an empty room. Marketing is how you fix that, and this course shows you how.

You’ll learn the fundamentals of marketing, explore what makes arts and culture marketing its own creative animal, and get comfortable with market research. You’ll also learn to build strategies that grow audiences and increase accessibility.

Through case studies, readings, lively discussion, and hands-on exercises using real organizations and this week’s headlines, you’ll practice turning first-time visitors into loyal fans.

You’ll leave with practical skills you can use right away and a new way of seeing every poster, email, and Instagram post that crosses your path.

No experience required. Curiosity mandatory.

What you’ll walk out with

  1. The fundamentals, for real

    Product, price, place, promotion. You’ll know the marketing mix well enough to explain it at a party, and to actually use it on Monday.

  2. A playbook built for the arts

    Selling a season is not selling sneakers. You’ll develop best practices that fit museums, theaters, orchestras, and the scrappy collective down the street.

  3. A feel for arts audiences

    Who shows up, who doesn’t, and why. You’ll learn to describe the people in the seats (and the ones you wish were there) with real nuance.

  4. Sharp analysis skills

    You’ll size up the competition and run a SWOT analysis that leads somewhere: a marketing plan with a point of view.

  5. Research you can trust

    Surveys, segments, metrics. You’ll practice the research and evaluation methods that let you say “here’s what the data shows” and mean it.

What you’ll make

No final exam. You pick a real arts organization, study it all semester, and build the kind of work you can show a future boss.

Your own website

You are a brand too. You’ll build a personal site and keep sharpening it as your marketing chops grow.

A public voice

LinkedIn posts and journal entries that get you thinking out loud and start building your arts management profile.

Three deep dives

Product and brand. Place, price, and positioning. Audience engagement and a full SWOT. All on the organization you chose.

A communications plan

The finale. Everything you’ve learned, turned into a plan your organization could actually pick up and run with.
