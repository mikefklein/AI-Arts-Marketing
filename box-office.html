Good questions

When and where does it meet?

Thursdays from 5:30 to 8 p.m. in the Katzen Arts Center, Room 112, during the fall semester. One session is a Tuesday morning field trip.

Do I have to buy a textbook?

No. All course materials are provided online through Canvas.

Is there a final exam?

The semester ends with a communications plan for a real arts organization, due in exam week. You’ll get there by way of three shorter analyses, a personal website, and a couple of LinkedIn posts or journal entries.

Is it in person?

Yes. Being in the room is a core part of the class. With prior approval, students may join up to three sessions virtually.

Can I use AI tools for my work?

For the early and technical stuff, yes: brainstorming, first-pass research, definitions, and proofreading what you’ve already written. The thinking and the writing are yours. That’s where the learning is.

What if a deadline goes sideways?

Ask early. Extensions are a conversation, and the best time to have it is before the due date, the same as in any good workplace.

Who do I talk to?

Marisa Klein, the instructor. Email mklein@american.edu to set up a time. For questions about the degree, start with AU’s Arts Management program.
