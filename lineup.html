How a night goes

  1. You arrive having done the reading

    Readings are the foundation. The good conversations are built on top of them.

  2. Somebody brings a find

    A poster, an email, an ad from the Metro. Marketing spotted in the wild is always welcome.

  3. We dig in

    Lecture, case studies, and real organizations making real decisions. Sometimes a guest takes the mic.

  4. You talk. And listen.

    Quality over quantity. Being a generous listener counts as much as having the hot take.
