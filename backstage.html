House rules

This room runs on belonging, thoughtful inquiry, and kindness. Here is what you can count on.

  • Each of you is an important and valued part of this community.
  • Your experiences and perspectives make the room better.
  • You have the right to determine and express your own identity.
  • Nobody is ever expected to represent an entire identity.
  • You are entitled to a safe space for your own learning.

Bring questions. Bring opinions. Bring that poster you can’t stop thinking about.

The making-of

How this site got made

  1. The class picked the brand

    Students took a quiz called “What’s Our Brand??” The verdict: excitement. A worn canvas tote. A flyer on a lamppost. Warm and witty.

  2. Marisa wrote a one-page brief

    Who it’s for, how it should look and sound, and what it must include, accessibility rules first.

  3. An AI model built it

    Working from the brief and the syllabus, as a live demo for a class session on AI in arts marketing. The tote bag logo is drawn in code.

  4. Humans checked it

    Facts, links, voice, and accessibility. The AI does the typing. People do the deciding.
